Our story

Technology that makes everyday life better

VoltCart began in 2019 with a simple idea: great gadgets shouldn't cost a fortune, and buying them online shouldn't feel risky. What started as two friends selling headphones from a spare bedroom is now one of the Philippines' fastest-growing online tech stores.

We carefully curate every product in our catalogue. Before an item earns a spot, our team tests it for build quality, battery life and real-world performance, so you don't have to gamble on reviews.

Today we serve thousands of students, creators, remote workers and travellers nationwide, and we are just getting started.

Our journey

From a Spare Room to Nationwide

A quick look at the milestones that shaped VoltCart.

2019

The idea

Two friends frustrated with overpriced, unreliable gadgets start selling headphones from a spare bedroom in Quezon City.

2021

Going online

VoltCart launches its first website and ships to all 81 provinces, introducing free shipping over ₱1,500.

2023

Our own warehouse

We move into a 600 sqm fulfilment centre in Makati, cutting average delivery time from 5 days to 2.

2025

10,000 happy customers

We reach 10,000 orders and add a 1-year warranty and 7-day return policy across the whole catalogue.

2026

What's next

New smart-home line, a mobile app and pick-up lockers in Metro Manila are coming soon.
